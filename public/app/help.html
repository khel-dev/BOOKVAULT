Frequently Asked Questions

How do I add a new client to my account?

To add a new client, go to the Dashboard and click the "add new clients" button, or navigate to the Clients page and click "Add Client". Fill in the required information including name, email, phone, and business type.

How do I generate and send invoices?

Navigate to the Billing Statement page, select your client, and click "Create Invoice". Fill in the services and amounts, then use the "Send Invoice" button to email it directly to your client.

How do I track overdue payments?

The Dashboard shows upcoming due dates, and the Notifications page will alert you to overdue payments. You can also set up automatic email reminders in Settings.

Can I customize my invoice templates?

Yes! Go to Settings > Business Settings > Invoice Templates to customize your invoice design, add your logo, and modify the layout to match your brand.

How do I backup my data?

BookVault automatically backs up your data daily. You can also enable manual backups in Settings > Data & Privacy, or export your data anytime for your own records.

What payment methods can I accept?

BookVault supports multiple payment methods including bank transfers, credit cards, PayPal, and digital wallets. Configure your preferred methods in Settings > Business Settings.
